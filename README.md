# 🚴‍♂️ Cyclistic Bike-Share Data Analysis
> **Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate - Capstone Project**

Welcome to my capstone project for the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate! In this case study, I analyzed 12 months of historical trip data from Cyclistic, a fictional bike-share company in Chicago.

## 🎯 Business Objective
The primary goal is to understand how **Annual Members** and **Casual Riders** use Cyclistic bikes differently, and to design data-driven marketing strategies to convert casual riders into loyal annual members.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Python (Pandas):** Data cleaning, feature engineering, and aggregation. (See `cyclistic_data_cleaning.py`)
* **Tableau Public:** Data visualization and interactive dashboard creation.
* **MS Word:** Comprehensive reporting. (See `CyclisticCaseStudy_Report.docx`)

## 📊 Key Insights
* **Ride Duration:** Casual riders take significantly longer trips (**21.1 mins** average) compared to Annual Members (**14.8 mins**).
* **Peak Hours (Commuting vs. Leisure):** Members show sharp usage peaks at **8:00 AM** and **5:00 PM** (commuters). Casual rider usage climbs steadily and peaks in the late afternoon.
* **Weekly Trends:** Member usage is highly consistent on weekdays, while Casual rider volume spikes massively on **Saturdays and Sundays**.

## 💡 Marketing Recommendations
1. **Weekend & Seasonal Passes:** Introduce a targeted "Weekend Annual Membership" or Summer Pass to capture the high volume of weekend casual riders.
2. **Duration-Based Incentives:** Offer membership discounts to users who consistently log rides longer than 20 minutes.
3. **Targeted Campaigns:** Launch digital ad campaigns at high-volume tourist and leisure stations during weekend peak hours (3 PM - 6 PM).
